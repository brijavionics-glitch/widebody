WIDEBODY TRACK Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026.

What WIDEBODY TRACK does

WIDEBODY TRACK shows public flight information for a list of aircraft at selected airports.

Information we keep

  1. A random ID made by the app on your phone, to count how many people are online. It is deleted after one hour of no use.
  2. A rough city found from your internet address, shown in the "online now" list. It is not your exact location.
  3. Team voice messages that authorised users record and send. Only the latest message for each airport is kept.
  4. Settings (airports and aircraft list) shared by all users.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you choose to record a team voice message.

What we do not do

  1. We do not ask for your name, phone number or email.
  2. We do not sell or share your information.
  3. We do not show ads.

Data sources

Flight data comes from public sources: Flightradar24, adsb.lol and adsb.fi.
