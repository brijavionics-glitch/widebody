❓ Help
  1. Mumbai Live and Delhi Live show every arrival and departure of our aircraft.
  2. Fleet shows where each aircraft is now.
  3. The orange line under an arrival shows how far the aircraft has flown.
  4. A red blinking time means the arrival is within 15 minutes, or the departure is within 1 hour 30 minutes.
  5. The team voice message plays once each time the app is opened (touch the screen once first).
  6. Tap an aircraft name to share it on WhatsApp.
  7. A * after Landed or Departed means the time has passed, but the source has not confirmed it yet.
  8. Data comes from Flightradar24, adsb.lol and adsb.fi.
